<product_requirements>
The Poker Chip Control application aims to be a comprehensive tool for managing poker game sessions, player chip transactions, and financial tracking for a poker club.
1.  **Game Session Management**: Create, manage, and close poker sessions. Sessions should load existing players with their carry-over balances. A critical aspect is that all players must cash out (even if /bin/bash) to close a game, with any remaining credit becoming debt for the next game.
2.  **Player Chip Operations**: Players can acquire chips via Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit. Chips acquired add to their Chips in Game. Players can divest chips via Cashed Out or Paid with Chips. Cashed Out converts chips to profit, setting credit balance to zero. Paid with Chips reduces current debt. A new Pay Credit operation is required to explicitly pay off existing player debt, ensuring they leave without pending debt.
3.  **Financial Tracking**:
    *   **Total Table**: Sum of all chips put into play (Cash, Transfer, Credit) minus chips taken out (Cashed Out, Paid with Chips).
    *   **Player Profit/Loss**: Calculated as Chips Taken Out - Chips Put In.
    *   **Club Earnings (NEW logic)**: The total chips remaining on the table after all players have cashed out or paid off their debts from a closed game represent the club's profit. This feature was previously implemented and then explicitly requested to be removed, stating the app should *not* track club commissions/earnings.
4.  **Reporting**: List past sessions, player reports showing chip transactions, and export data.
5.  **User Experience (UX)**:
    *   Intuitive player selection for new games.
    *   Transaction buttons (3x2 grid).
    *   Larger back button touch target.
    *   Display Total Table Amount and per-player Chips in Game.
    *   Blue application theme.
    *   Bilingual support (Spanish/English).
    *   Player Management: Edit/delete players, add/display circular profile photos. Photos should display in game screen and player manager.
    *   Logo integration: PokerChipControl text with provided logo.
    *   Money formatting: Remove .00 and add commas for thousands (e.g., ,000).
    *   Simplified Dashboard: Only Debt Owed and Club Earnings (this was later reverted, Club Earnings to be removed completely).
    *   Compact Player UI in Game Screen: Player balance displayed next to name, started, and chips in game.
    *   Button text: Add Transaction button to be simplified to ADD.
</product_requirements>
