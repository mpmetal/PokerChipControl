The Poker Chip Control application is a comprehensive tool for managing poker game sessions, player transactions, and club finances. It allows users to create, manage, and close game sessions, enforcing that all players cash out before closure. Players handle chips via Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit, and divest through Cashed Out, Paid with Chips, or Pay Credit. Financial tracking includes Total Table (chips in play), Player Profit/Loss, and Club Earnings. The app supports reporting and CSV export of past sessions. UX emphasizes intuitive, thumb-friendly design, 3x2 transaction grid, large back buttons, blue theme, and bilingual support (Spanish/English). Player management includes circular Base64 profile photos. Money formatting is clean. The dashboard displays Debt Owed and Club Earnings.
