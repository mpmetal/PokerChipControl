<product_requirements>
The Poker Chip Control application provides a comprehensive solution for managing poker game sessions, player transactions, and financial tracking for poker clubs. It enables users to create, manage, and close game sessions, requiring all players to cash out before a game can be closed. The system prevents closure if players have positive balances. Players can acquire chips through Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit, and divest them via Cashed Out, Paid with Chips, or Pay Credit. Financial tracking includes Total Table (chips in play), Player Profit/Loss, and Club Earnings (total chips on table across active games). The application also supports reporting of past sessions and player transactions with CSV export. The UX emphasizes intuitive design with a 3x2 transaction button grid, larger back buttons, a blue theme, bilingual support (Spanish/English), and player management with circular profile photos stored as Base64. Money formatting should be clean, and the dashboard displays Debt Owed and Club Earnings. The game screen features a compact player UI with photo, name, balance, Chips in Game, and Total Table Amount. Visual indicators confirm cashed-out players.
</product_requirements>
