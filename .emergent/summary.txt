<product_requirements>
The Poker Chip Control application aims to be a comprehensive tool for managing poker game sessions, player chip transactions, and financial tracking for a poker club.
1.  **Game Session Management**: Users can create, manage, and close poker sessions. Players must cash out (even if /bin/bash balance) to close a game, with any remaining credit becoming debt. The system prevents closing a game if players have positive balances (chips still in play).
2.  **Player Chip Operations**: Players acquire chips via Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit (adding to Chips in Game). They divest chips via Cashed Out (converting chips to profit, setting credit balance to zero), Paid with Chips (reducing debt), or the newly implemented Pay Credit (explicitly paying off debt without affecting Total Table).
3.  **Financial Tracking**:
    *   **Total Table**: Sum of all chips put into play (Cash, Transfer, Credit) minus chips taken out (Cashed Out, Paid with Chips).
    *   **Player Profit/Loss**: Calculated as Chips Taken Out - Chips Put In. Player balance interpretation: positive balance means Owed to Player (organizer owes player), negative means Player Owes (player owes organizer).
    *   **Club Earnings**: Displayed on the Home Screen, representing the total chips remaining on the table across all active games. The app should *not* track club commissions or percentage-based earnings logic.
4.  **Reporting**: Ability to list past sessions and player transaction reports, with CSV export.
5.  **User Experience (UX)**: Intuitive player selection, 3x2 transaction button grid, larger back button, blue theme, bilingual support (Spanish/English). Player management includes edit/delete players with circular profile photos (Base64 storage) displayed in game and player manager. Money formatting removes .00 and adds commas. Dashboard shows Debt Owed and Club Earnings. Game screen displays compact player UI (photo, name, balance on one line), Chips in Game, and Total Table Amount. Add Transaction button text simplified to ADD. Visual indicators (checkmark, CASHED OUT label) for players who have cashed out.
</product_requirements>
